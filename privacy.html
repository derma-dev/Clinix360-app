Privacy Policy — DSkin Instagram Assistant

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Derma Skin and Hair Solutions ("DSkin", "we", "us"), Delhi, India, handles your information when you message us or comment on our posts on Instagram. Our Instagram messages are first answered by an automated assistant that runs on the Clinix360 app. Cloudus Infotech Private Limited (Surat, Gujarat, India) runs the app for us and processes your information only on our instructions.

In short:
  • An automated assistant may reply to your messages first. Type "human" at any time to talk to our team.
  • We use what you send us only to answer your enquiry and to arrange a call back or a visit.
  • We keep chats for 12 months after your last message, then delete them.
  • We don't sell your information. To see or delete it, email hospitalitybee@gmail.com.

1. The automated assistant

  • When you message us, an automated assistant powered by artificial intelligence may reply first. It says so in its first message.
  • It answers questions about our services, prices and branches, and collects the details our team needs to call you back or book your visit. It never confirms an appointment itself: our team does that.
  • It does not give medical advice. Messages about symptoms, medicines or problems after a treatment are passed to our staff.
  • To talk to a person, type "human", "real person" or "staff se baat karao" at any time. Once our staff reply in a chat, the assistant stops replying in that chat.
  • Prices and offers the assistant quotes are indicative. The final price is confirmed at your consultation, and an offer applies only for the period stated in its post.
  • Instagram is not for emergencies. If you have a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

2. Information we collect

  • From Instagram: your Instagram user ID for our account, your username and display name, the messages you send us, the comments you post on our posts, and the post you messaged or commented about.
  • What you tell us in the chat: your phone or WhatsApp number, your area, preferred branch, preferred day and time, and the service you are interested in.
  • Anything else you write, including details about your skin, hair or health if you choose to share them. Please don't send medical details on Instagram: our doctors will discuss them with you at your consultation.
  • What we record about the chat: a short summary, the service and price we quoted, and why the chat was passed to our staff.

We do not collect payment details through Instagram.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to you and answer questions about our services, prices and offers.
  • To pass your enquiry to our team so they can call you back or book your visit, by phone or WhatsApp on the number you gave us.
  • To check the quality of the assistant's replies, and to count enquiries in our internal reports.

You choose to message us, and we use what you send only for your enquiry (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, section 7(a)). We do not sell your information, use it for advertising or profiling, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

4. Who processes your information

These service providers process your information on our behalf, only to run this service:

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram) delivers your messages and comments to us. Instagram's own privacy policy applies to what you post on Instagram.
  • Google (Gemini API, paid service) writes the assistant's replies and receives the chat text to do so. Google does not use it to improve its products, and keeps it for a limited time only to detect misuse.
  • Supabase hosts the database that stores chats and enquiries.
  • Netlify hosts the app.
  • Telegram carries alerts to our clinic owner: your name and your question when the assistant needs an answer from us or passes your chat to staff.
  • Make (make.com) delivers your enquiry (name, number, service, branch, preferred time and the chat) to our clinic's own enquiry system.
  • Resend sends alert emails to our team, for example when Telegram can't be reached.
  • Cloudus Infotech Private Limited builds and runs the app, and accesses your information only to operate and fix it.

Some of these providers store or process information outside India. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Chats and enquiry records in this app: 12 months after your last message, then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • If you become our patient, your records are kept under our clinic's medical records practice.
  • Instagram keeps its own copy of the chat in our Instagram inbox, under Instagram's rules.

6. How we protect it

  • Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Only signed-in clinic and app staff can open the stored chats, and our Instagram access key is kept on the server, never in the browser.
  • If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and who we shared it with;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it (see Data deletion);
  • stop using it: you can withdraw at any time, as easily as you gave it;
  • let someone you nominate exercise these rights if you die or can't act yourself.

Email hospitalitybee@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We may ask you to confirm the request from the same Instagram account, and we reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Data deletion

To delete your information, email hospitalitybee@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the Instagram username you used to message us. Within 30 days we delete your chats and enquiry records from this app and from our clinic's enquiry system, and confirm by email. Records we must keep by law (for example, the records of a treatment you had with us) are kept only as long as the law requires.

9. Children

This service is meant for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us. If we learn that we have collected a child's information without a parent's consent, we delete it.

10. Contact and Grievance Officer

Questions or complaints about this policy or your information: Grievance Officer, Derma Skin and Hair Solutions, hospitalitybee@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
