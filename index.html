Connected Accounts

Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp accounts feeding the Leads inbox. Turn one off to pause ingesting new messages from that platform. Existing leads and history stay untouched.

Automations

Comment Automation

When someone comments on an Instagram post or a Facebook Page post, reply publicly under their comment and send them one DM. End the DM with the branch question: a tappable button per branch is added automatically, and their answer routes the lead and lifts the platform’s send limit so staff can reply freely. The same rules apply to both platforms. Name the branches in the text too: buttons don’t show on desktop. First matching keyword wins; use * to catch every comment.

Chatbot (Instagram DMs)

The Instagram DM assistant answers price and FAQ questions, qualifies leads and hands the conversation to staff. Mode is the master switch: Off does nothing, Shadow drafts replies but sends nothing (logged for review), Live replies to customers. Saved replies below are what the bot actually says.
